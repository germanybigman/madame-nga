# Maintenance Agreement Template

## 1. Scope

This document outlines a simple support arrangement for the website and admin system after handover.

## 2. Included Services

The developer may provide the following services as an optional ongoing support plan:

- minor content updates
- menu edits and pricing changes
- image updates
- bug fixes related to the deployed site
- deployment support if the host or domain needs adjustment
- occasional improvements or small design tweaks

## 3. Excluded Services

The following are not included unless explicitly agreed in writing:

- major redesigns
- custom backend development
- complete rebuilds or feature development
- complex legal/compliance review
- third-party support work unrelated to the website

## 4. Payment Terms

- One-time handoff/setup fee: €[amount]
- Monthly maintenance fee: €[amount] per month
- Support requests may be billed at €[amount] per hour if outside the maintenance plan

## 5. Responsibilities

The restaurant remains responsible for:

- domain ownership and DNS settings
- hosting account access
- final legal review of website copy and policies
- admin credentials and staff access
- all business decisions related to the content and menu

## 6. Support Response

Support requests will be handled during normal business hours unless otherwise agreed.

## 7. Termination

Either party may terminate this maintenance agreement with notice.
